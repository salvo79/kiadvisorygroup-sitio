What we do

Four capabilities. One cycle.

We support the full cycle: deciding what is worth doing, turning it into an executable program, putting it into operation and measuring that the numbers are met. Every phase delivers something you use the next day.

01

Strategic planning

We define what to produce, sell, buy and invest in with optimization (LP) models that balance demand, capacity, inventory and financial targets.

  • Optimization model and scenarios
  • Optimal plan with targets by period
  • Sensitivity and shadow price of every constraint
02

Operational scheduling

We turn the plan into concrete programs: targets by person, zone and month; routes, shifts and orders; all agreed with the people who carry them out.

  • Program by team, zone and period
  • Allocation by capacity and track record
  • Target agreement with each owner
03

Operations implementation

We put the program into operation: processes, roles, PMO, dashboards and the platform that supports it, with partners present in the field.

  • Process and governance design (PMO)
  • Infinity Workflow rollout
  • Training and adoption in the field
04

Evaluation and follow-up

We measure actuals against plan, explain the gaps and replan. We do not close a project until the numbers confirm it.

  • Real-time KPI dashboards
  • Monthly, quarterly and annual reviews
  • Replanning that keeps closed months
